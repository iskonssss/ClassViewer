ClassView Keys

Closing this window keeps the overlay running.
Allow ClassView Keys to see your keyboard and mouse. On a Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → turn on ClassView Keys. Then click Restart.
Click Record and press the keys (or click / drag / scroll the mouse) to fill a shortcut, or type it like Ctrl+Shift+G, Shift+Right Drag, Scroll. The label is optional: leave it empty to show just the keys. Ctrl also matches Cmd on a Mac (on Windows, Cmd means the Windows key).
